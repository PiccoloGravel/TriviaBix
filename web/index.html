📺 On the TV

The TV runs itself: it opens a room and shows the code, and phones do the rest. Open this on the TV once and save it:
Cast from an Android phone (Galaxy)
  1. Get the TriviaBix app and install it (allow installs from your browser when Android asks).
  2. Open it, then turn on Smart View and pick the TV.
  3. The TV shows the game with sound, and the phone stays your controller.
Samsung or other smart TV browser
  1. Open the TV's Internet app and type the address above.
  2. Bookmark it (or add it to the home screen). Press OK once on the page to turn sound on.
Fire TV, Google TV, Android TV
  1. Install a browser app: Silk (Fire TV), or Fully Kiosk Browser, which also plays sound without the first click.
  2. Open the address above and set it as the start page (or bookmark it).
  3. Next time, just open the app.
Chromecast, Apple TV, or no browser
  1. Use a spare phone or tablet as the TV: tap the button below on it.
  2. Mirror its screen: Google Home → Cast my screen on Android, Screen Mirroring on iPhone with an Apple TV.
  3. Turn the phone sideways and keep it plugged in. Everyone plays on their own phones.
