Bix's characters

Cameos: sometimes · change

Now and then Bix breaks into one of these characters for a line. On the TV he wears the costume, his speech bubble says who he's playing, and the line plays at the character's speed. Some characters shift the pitch too. The line writer gives questions a cameo where the topic fits, and the mini games and big moments have their own. How often he does it is under Settings → Game show → Character cameos.
