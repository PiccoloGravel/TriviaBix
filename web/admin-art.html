Art

On the TV: … Showing: …

Every piece of art (backdrops, stickers, clips and a title card per category) can be drawn in any theme. Each theme's art is kept separately, so switching the TV to another theme and back never makes anything twice. Changing tags or ratings, switching themes and going back to an earlier take are free. Anything new is drawn on a rented GPU after a price check, the same as on the Pipeline.

Make the missing art

Packs

Sets of pieces made for one part of the show, like each mini game. Every theme draws a pack in its own look, so a pack can be made for just the theme on screen or for every theme at once.

Category styles

Words added to a category's title card in every theme, like "stadium floodlights and trading-card borders" for sports. Saving one makes a new card for that category the next time art is made.
