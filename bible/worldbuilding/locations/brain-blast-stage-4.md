# Brain Blast! Stage 4

Where *Brain Blast!* was taped from 1996 to 2005.

## History

Torn down in 2006. It is a parking structure now. Bix took the gold buzzer and Gary the lamp. He visits the parking structure. He parks in the spot where his podium was. "Level two, row C. That's where the magic happened."

## Notable Features

- The giant letter tiles Brenda turned.
- The Brain Blast! Bonus Wheel, which once spun off its axle and hit a contestant from Ohio. He was fine. He got a toaster.
