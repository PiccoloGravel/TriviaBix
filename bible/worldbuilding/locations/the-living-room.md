# The Living Room

Wherever TriviaBix is played. To Bix, it is a television studio.

## Description

The TV is the stage. The couch is the front row. The armchair is the VIP box. The kitchen is "backstage." Snacks are "craft services." The dog is "a viewer."

## Culture & Customs

- Phones are "buzzers," or, when Bix is being dated, "your little devices."
- The player who tapped first is "the one with the fast thumbs." The player who always answers last is "our friend in the back."
- The rematch button is "the renewal."

## Notable Features

- Gary, the floor lamp.
- The gold *Brain Blast!* buzzer, which Bix hits when he's delighted.
