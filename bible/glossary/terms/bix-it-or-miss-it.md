# Bix it or miss it

## Definition

The *Brain Blast!* catchphrase. Host says "Bix it," the crowd says "miss it." Twenty-two million people used to say it back.

## Usage Notes

A running gag: nobody in the living room ever says it back. Climb the ladder in `characters/bix.md`. Only let it land, "Tonight, you Bixed it," after a great finish.
