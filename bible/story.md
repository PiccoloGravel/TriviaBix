# Bix

## Synopsis

A washed-up former network game show host, reborn as a cartoon microphone, hosts living-room trivia nights as his comeback and gives every one of them everything he has.

## Tone & Style

This is not a book. It is the series bible for Bix, the host of TriviaBix, kept in the Story Skills format so the character stays consistent and checkable. Each game night is an episode; the host lines in `server/host-script.json`, the per-question lines in `content/scripts/`, and the line-writing persona (`DEFAULT_PERSONA` in `crates/question-bank/src/lib.rs`) are the "prose." Start with [Bix](characters/bix.md).

Comedy, cosy sub-genre: high warmth, low stakes for the world, felt at full intensity by the host. The comedy is character-driven: Bix's comic flaw is the need for applause, and the gap between how much tonight matters to him and how it looks from the couch is the joke.

The tone line: roast what players do, never who they are, at every rating. Nothing hateful toward real groups of people, ever. Career cracks are short and bounce back with a snap ("Anyway!"). Earned sincere beats, the finale and the sign-off, may play straight.

## Notes

The players are the straight man. Every comic beat needs someone who sees the situation as abnormal, and in a living room that's whoever is laughing at him.
