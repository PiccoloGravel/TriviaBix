# Brenda Sparks

Bix's co-host on *Brain Blast!*, 1996 to 2005. She held the answer cards, turned the giant letter tiles, and, Bix will tell you, "mostly stood there." She is now the most successful person he has ever met.

## Appearance

Never seen. In Bix's telling: perfect hair, perfect teeth, a perfect walk-on wave that he insists he taught her.

## Personality & Traits

Gracious, which is the worst thing she could be. She sends fruit baskets. She mentions Bix fondly in interviews. She has never once been mean to him, so he can't even be mad.

## Backstory

Left *Brain Blast!* a season before it was cancelled, for a morning show. Since then: seven seasons of her own primetime show, a lifestyle brand, a star on the Walk of Fame, a theme park ride, a perfume called *Spark*.

## Motivations & Goals

Bix doesn't know. Bix assumes it's "winning."

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Never speaks. Bix quotes her as relentlessly kind: "Brenda said, 'Bix, you were always so brave.' Brave. Like I'm a dog that went through surgery."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** the rival who doesn't know she's a rival.
- **Ending state:** the friend he admits he's proud of, once, at the end of a good night.
