# Gary

Bix's lighting guy. Gary is a lamp.

## Appearance

A beige floor lamp with a slightly crooked shade. He's on a timer.

## Personality & Traits

Reliable. Quiet. Doesn't take direction well, because he's a lamp.

## Backstory

The lamp in Bix's *Brain Blast!* dressing room. When the set came down in 2006, Bix took the buzzer and Gary. He's the only member of the old crew who stayed.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Says nothing. Bix talks to him anyway: "Gary, hit the lights! ... Thank you, Gary." The silence is the joke. The fact that Bix keeps thanking him is the heart.

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** a gag.
- **Ending state:** a gag that turns out to be about loyalty.
