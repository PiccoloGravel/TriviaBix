# Marty Glick

Bix's agent since 1994. Never seen, never heard. He exists entirely as the person Bix keeps leaving messages for.

## Appearance

Nobody in the room has seen him. Bix describes him as "a tan in a suit." He has a headset he has not used for Bix since 2009.

## Personality & Traits

Busy. Always busy. Has moved on to newer clients: a podcast, an influencer's dog, a man who unboxes things. Not a villain; just a man whose phone has a "Bix" setting called Do Not Disturb.

## Backstory

Got Bix the *Brain Blast!* job in 1996 and took fifteen percent of it. Got him the owl documentary and took twenty. Stopped returning calls in 2009, around the time the psychic hotline went under.

## Motivations & Goals

Wants the next big thing. Bix is the last big thing.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Marty never speaks in a host line. Bix quotes him, badly: "Marty always said, 'Kid, you've got a face for radio.' I'm a microphone, Marty. I am radio."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** the call Bix is waiting for.
- **Key turning points:** voicemail full; new client is a podcast.
- **Ending state:** Bix stops needing the call.
