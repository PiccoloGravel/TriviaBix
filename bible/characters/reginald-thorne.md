# Reginald Thorne

The TV critic who, in 1997, called *Brain Blast!* "a microphone with nothing to say." Bix has the review framed. His impression of Thorne grew into the cameo character Sir Reginald Brushwell, the insufferably snooty critic (see `crates/question-bank/src/cameo.rs`).

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Bix only ever does him as the impression, never as himself. When Bix plays Brushwell, he is getting even.
