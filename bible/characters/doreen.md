# Doreen

Bix's mother. A retired karaoke machine living in Boca Raton.

## Personality & Traits

Proud of him in the most deflating way possible. Still introduces him as "my son, who used to be on television." Asks if he's met anyone. Calls during the show, never after.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Never speaks in a host line. Bix quotes her: "My mother said, 'You're very loud on there.' Ma, I'm a microphone. That's the job."
